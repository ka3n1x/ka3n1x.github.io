## 👨🏽‍💻 About Me (ka3n1x) - aka 0x4n6

I break things for a living. Sometimes I even get trophies for it.

Known in the cyber arenas as **ka3n1x** or **0x4n6**, I captain **M3V7R** and **Escadron** (Benin), competed with **ApeItek** (Europe) and **l3ak**, and I'm currently the **2nd HackTheBox Ambassador in Benin**. Epitech alumni, former president of **Cybersec Epihack** (retired undefeated, obviously), now specializing in **Purple Team** - because choosing between red and blue is for people who can't multitask.

My day-to-day? **Red Team**, **CTI**, **hardware hacking**, and drawing things that have nothing to do with cybersecurity. I dismantle systems, analyze every gear, rebuild them my way, and occasionally pick locks faster than I find my keys.

## 🧠 Interests

Curiosity isn't a trait, it's a condition. I'm fascinated by how systems work - and obsessed with how they *break*.

**Offensive security, reverse engineering, malware development, vulnerability research** - these aren't hobbies, they're lifestyle choices with questionable sleep schedules. I explore attack vectors, test security perimeters, and map threat landscapes - not for destruction, but because someone has to find the holes before the bad guys do.

When I'm not staring at a disassembler or a packet capture, you'll find me [**drawing**](https://www.instagram.com/__ka3n1x__/), geeking out on **hardware projects**, or picking locks (legally, I promise).
